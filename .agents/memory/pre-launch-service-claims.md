Treat the supplied human-listening story as the intended future service, not evidence of established operational practices. Preserve its meaning and the closing line “HABLAR — Come as you are. Speak as you feel.” without promising listener anonymity, confidentiality, outcomes, or absolute safety.

The requested future-service direction is that a participant's identity would be anonymous to the person they connect with, and their first interaction would take place on HABLAR. This is a product intention, not evidence that the flow or identity controls exist. Be explicit that it is not offered by the coming-soon site and does not mean anonymity from HABLAR.

**Why:** The supplied brand copy describes anonymous conversations and account/payment handling, but the task explicitly requires landing summaries to match verified legal disclosures and forbids changing actual privacy, listener, or safety practices.

**How to apply:** Check the current Privacy Notice and Terms before adapting marketing copy. Keep these benefits conditional and future-facing, and verify the actual identity visibility and interaction flow before presenting them as live capabilities.
