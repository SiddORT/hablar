Treat the supplied human-listening story as the intended future service, not evidence of established operational practices. Preserve its meaning and the closing line “HABLAR — Come as you are. Speak as you feel.” without promising listener anonymity, confidentiality, outcomes, or absolute safety.

**Why:** The supplied brand copy describes anonymous conversations and account/payment handling, but the task explicitly requires landing summaries to match verified legal disclosures and forbids changing actual privacy, listener, or safety practices.

**How to apply:** Check the current Privacy Notice and Terms before adapting marketing copy. Distinguish aspirations from live capabilities, and only introduce stronger claims once the underlying practices and disclosures are confirmed.
